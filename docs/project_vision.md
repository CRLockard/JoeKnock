# JoeKnock - Project Vision

## Overview

JoeKnock is a lightweight field interaction platform designed for organizations that rely on teams working directly with people in the field.

While the initial focus is door-to-door sales, JoeKnock is designed to support any organization that needs to quickly capture location-based interactions, including:

- Door-to-door sales teams
- Community outreach organizations
- HOA engagement teams
- Political canvassing groups
- Signature collection campaigns
- Field survey teams

JoeKnock is built around one core idea:

> Capture the moment. Don't interrupt the work.

The goal is to give field users a simple, fast way to record what happened at a location while keeping their attention focused on the interaction itself.

---

# Problem Statement

Organizations that rely on field teams often lose valuable information between the moment an interaction happens and when that information reaches the rest of the business.

Common challenges include:

- Representatives forgetting important details after completing their route
- Paper notes being lost or incomplete
- Delayed data entry causing inaccurate information
- Managers lacking visibility into field activity
- Important opportunities being missed because information does not move quickly

Existing CRM platforms often focus on managing the customer lifecycle after an opportunity has already been identified.

However, the critical moment before that happens—the initial field interaction—is often where valuable information is lost.

---

# Solution

JoeKnock provides field users with a map-first workspace where they can quickly:

- Identify a location
- Record the outcome of an interaction
- Add relevant notes
- Continue to the next opportunity

The information collected is immediately available to the organization, allowing managers and administrators to maintain visibility into field activity.

JoeKnock does not attempt to replace a CRM.

Instead, it captures the field interaction and allows organizations to send that information to their existing systems through exports and future integrations.

JoeKnock does not determine:

- Lead ownership
- Sales assignment
- Commission responsibility
- Customer lifecycle

Those decisions remain with the organization's existing systems and processes.

---

# Product Philosophy

## The Representative Comes First

The success of JoeKnock depends on adoption by the people using it every day.

If the field user cannot quickly and easily capture information, the system fails.

The application prioritizes:

- Speed
- Minimal typing
- Simple workflows
- Clear visual feedback
- Mobile and tablet usability

The user should not feel like they are completing paperwork.

They should feel like they are continuing their work.

---

## JoeKnock Is Not a CRM

JoeKnock is intentionally focused.

It does not attempt to manage:

- Long-term customer relationships
- Sales pipelines
- Revenue forecasting
- Full customer lifecycle management

Instead, JoeKnock focuses on the field interaction itself.

It answers:

> What happened, where did it happen, and who recorded it?

The organization decides what happens next.

---

## Historical Accuracy Builds Trust

Every interaction matters.

JoeKnock preserves information about:

- Who performed an interaction
- When it occurred
- What information was captured
- What updates or corrections occurred afterward

The goal is to create confidence in the data.

Organizations should be able to understand what happened in the field without turning JoeKnock into a full CRM.

---

# Primary User

## Sales Representative

The primary user of JoeKnock is the field representative.

Their workflow should be simple:

1. Open the map.
2. Approach a location.
3. Record the outcome.
4. Move on.

The representative should have everything needed in the moment the interaction occurs.

Other roles, including managers and administrators, may also perform field interactions when needed.

---

# Secondary Users

## Managers

Managers benefit from visibility into team activity.

They can use JoeKnock to understand:

- Where their team is working
- What interactions are occurring
- How field activity is progressing

---

## Administrators

Administrators manage the organization experience.

They can:

- Manage users
- Configure teams
- Define statuses
- Control visibility settings
- Access organization-level information

---

# Core Product Statement

JoeKnock helps organizations capture critical field interactions at the moment they happen, providing representatives with a simple workflow and giving decision makers accurate visibility into what is happening in the field.

The goal is simple:

> Knock. Click. Go.
